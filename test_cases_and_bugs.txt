SAMPLE BUG REPORTS

Bug 1: Cart Counter Error
- Description: When adding an item to the cart, the cart badge icon does not update immediately.
- Steps to Reproduce: 
  1. Open products page.
  2. Click "Add to cart" on Backpack.
  3. Look at cart icon at top right.
- Expected Result: Cart icon shows "1".
- Actual Result: Cart icon stays at "0" until page is refreshed.
